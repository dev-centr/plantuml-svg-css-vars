= Actor stick-figure edge paints
:description: Bind PlantUML stickmen to edge tokens and classify actor paints.
:page-audience: plantuml-svg-css-vars users and maintainers
:page-usage-context: Understanding why actor stick figures vanished in host dark mode
:page-orig-author: Cursor agent on behalf of Ryan Johnson
:page-last-author: Cursor agent on behalf of Ryan Johnson
:page-last-edited: 2026-09-15

== Why

PlantUML `actor` stick figures are an ellipse head plus a `fill="none"` limb
path under `.participant`. v0.1.1 rebound those strokes to
`color.border.primary`. On host dark themes (Valentus), border is often a
near-invisible hairline (`rgba(255,255,255,0.09)`), so limbs and head outlines
disappeared while boxed participants still looked fine (fill-driven). Dashed
lifelines were also mis-tagged as `sequence-divider` when their stroke matched
divider black.

== Change

* Stick-figure ellipse / path / circle / line strokes bind to `color.edge`.
* `classifyPlantumlSequencePaint()` tags classic stickman ellipse + limb paths
  with `actor`, and skips `stroke-dasharray` lines when labeling dividers.
